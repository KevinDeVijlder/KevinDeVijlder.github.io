About me

Security through curiosity and continuous learning.

I'm Kevin, also known as Owly, a cybersecurity specialist with a passion for understanding how systems work, identifying weaknesses, and helping make the digital world a safer place. My interests span across offensive and defensive security, continuous learning, and staying up to date with the ever-evolving cybersecurity landscape.

Outside of my professional work, I actively invest time in developing my skills through hands-on labs and practical challenges on TryHackMe and Hack The Box to sharpen my technical abilities, explore new attack techniques, and gain deeper insight into real-world security scenarios.

I also document my learning journey by publishing write-ups and technical articles on Medium, sharing lessons learned, walkthroughs, and cybersecurity insights with the broader community.
